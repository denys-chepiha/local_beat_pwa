# Mobile video controls — 2026-10-23

The phone player now separates the picture from its controls. A single row below
the timeline contains play/pause, playlist, settings and fullscreen. Previous/next
and skip buttons remain on desktop; phones retain timeline and double-tap seeking.
Speed, repeat and mute/volume are available in an expandable settings panel.

The playlist opens below the controls on phones, with bounded scrolling and
wrapping filenames. Opening either panel closes the other. Selecting an entry,
including the active entry, closes the playlist. Escape dismisses a panel and
returns keyboard focus to its toggle.

Inline phone controls remain visible. Fullscreen controls hide while playing and
release their space to the picture; a tap restores them. Full-window fallback
keeps the app playlist available when container fullscreen is unavailable on iOS.
