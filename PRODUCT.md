# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Local Beat plays the user's local music, audiobooks and video without accounts or media uploads.

## Operating Context
MacBook and iPad, including tablet portrait and landscape. Local files and externally attached storage accessible through the system file picker.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Music and audiobooks persist locally. The approved Video section uses fullscreen controls with automatic hiding and a session-only playlist without copying videos into the library. Preserve the current visual style and use shared navigation. Keep the existing React/TypeScript stack.
