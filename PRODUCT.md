# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Local Beat plays the user's local music, audiobooks, books and video without accounts or media uploads.

## Operating Context
MacBook and iPad, including tablet portrait and landscape. Local files and externally attached storage accessible through the system file picker.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Music and audiobooks persist locally. The approved Video section uses fullscreen controls with automatic hiding and a device-local persistent playlist, per-video playback positions and watched markers. Preserve the current visual style and use shared navigation. Keep the existing React/TypeScript stack.

## Reader
A persistent local cover-card library supports PDF, EPUB, FB2, TXT, Markdown, HTML, DOCX and raster images. Reading is read-only, with paged/swipe and continuous modes, search, bookmarks, fullscreen and paper/sepia/night tones. The ordered sections are Music, Audio Books, Reader, Video; startup opens Music. Existing visual identity and shared media progress controls are retained.
